# Axeom Challenge — answers.json Field Specification & Mapping

This document provides a field-by-field specification of the original `answers.json` template. It defines the intended business meaning, structural shape, required evidence sources, calculation/verification methods, and resolves template ambiguities.

---

## Template Ambiguity & Placeholder Convention Notice

> [!WARNING]
> **Template Placeholder vs. Ground Truth**:
> In the original `answers.json` template, numeric fields (such as `client_count: 0`, `overdue_total: 0`, `never_billed_total: 0`, and list object amounts) contain the literal number `0`.
> - These are **illustrative template placeholders**, NOT measured findings.
> - A value of `0` in the template must not be interpreted as a proven zero balance or zero count.
> - Unsupported or uncalculated fields must remain unfilled during planning. In Phase 3, each field will be populated strictly from verified analytical proof.
> - Structural types (strings, lists, objects, integers, numbers) and key names must be preserved exactly as defined in the template.

---

## Detailed Field Specification

| Field Path | Original Template Type & Shape | Business Meaning & Description | Required Evidence Source(s) | Calculation & Verification Method | Known Ambiguities / Special Handling |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `pack_id` | `string` (`"PUT YOUR PACK NUMBER HERE (see PACK.txt)"`) | Unique challenge dataset identifier assigned to the candidate. | `PACK.txt` | Read raw text from `PACK.txt`. | Must be formatted as `"01"`, retaining the leading zero as text. Do not convert to integer `1`. |
| `your_name` | `string` (`""`) | Full legal or professional name of the candidate submitting the challenge. | Candidate submission metadata | User provided string. | Retain as string. |
| `your_email` | `string` (`""`) | Primary contact email address of the candidate. | Candidate submission metadata | User provided string. | Must be a valid email format. |
| `live_link` | `string` (`""`) | Publicly accessible URL hosting the running web application. Must work on mobile without requiring authentication. | Hosting platform deployment (e.g., PythonAnywhere, Render) | HTTP GET verification from an external unauthenticated browser. | Hard-to-guess URL, no login wall, responsive on mobile viewports. |
| `video_link` | `string` (`""`) | URL to the recorded 5–10 minute demonstration video. | Loom or cloud video hosting platform | Video playback verification and accessibility check. | Unauthenticated viewing access; must include unedited responses to the 3 mandatory questions with camera on. |
| `code_or_files_link` | `string` (`""`) | Link to public code repository or accessible shared folder containing complete project assets. | GitHub / GitLab / Google Drive | URL accessibility verification. | Must exclude secrets, `.env`, and virtual environment binaries. |
| `client_count` | `number` (`0`) | Total number of distinct, real active/historical clients served by Axeom. | `bookings.csv`, `invoices.csv`, `production_board.csv`, `leads.csv`, `messages.txt` | Deduplicated count of canonical client entities resolved by identity mapping. | Template default `0` is a placeholder. Does not include third-party payers or leads who never booked/purchased. |
| `clients` | `array of objects` (`[{"name": "", "other_names_used_in_the_data": [], "emails": [], "phones": []}]`) | Register of all canonical clients, listing canonical primary name, observed alias variants, email addresses, and phone numbers. | All files in `data/` | Discovered name variants, email addresses, and phone numbers clustered under verified canonical entities. | Conservative linking: names or shared phones alone do not force a merge without corroborating evidence (e.g., invoices/bookings linking them). |
| `payers_who_are_not_clients` | `array of objects` (`[{"name": "", "pays_for": []}]`) | Individuals or companies that appear in payment records paying for services, but who are not the client receiving the service. | `payments.csv` reconciled against `invoices.csv` and `bookings.csv` | Cross-match payer names/emails in `payments.csv` against bill-to and booking contacts. | Entities that pay on behalf of clients (e.g., corporate sponsors, parent companies, relatives) must be separated from actual clients. |
| `overdue` | `array of objects` (`[{"client": "", "invoice": "INV-0000", "amount_owed": 0, "days_late": 0}]`) | Line-by-line itemization of invoices with outstanding balances that are past their due date as of October 6, 2026. | `invoices.csv`, `payments.csv` | For each invoice: `amount_owed = invoice.amount - sum(matching_payments)`. If `amount_owed > 0` and `invoice.due < 2026-10-06`, `days_late = (2026-10-06 - invoice.due).days`. | `amount_owed: 0` and `days_late: 0` are template placeholders. Partially paid invoices must report remaining net balance, not original gross amount. |
| `overdue_total` | `number` (`0`) | Sum of all outstanding overdue amounts. | Computed from `overdue` array | $\sum \text{amount\_owed}$ across all `overdue` records. | Must reconcile exactly to the sum of line items in `overdue`. Template `0` is a placeholder. |
| `we_owe` | `array of objects` (`[{"client": "", "amount": 0, "why": ""}]`) | Monies Axeom owes back to clients (e.g. overpayments, refunds, deposits for unperformed work). | `payments.csv`, `invoices.csv`, `messages.txt`, `bookings.csv` | Audit of unapplied cash receipts, negative balances, and cancellation refund agreements. | Must detail the precise business cause in `why`. Template `amount: 0` is a placeholder. |
| `never_billed` | `array of objects` (`[{"client": "", "booking_id_or_month": "", "amount": 0}]`) | Chargeable work completed by the studio for which no invoice was ever issued. | `bookings.csv`, `production_board.csv` cross-referenced with `invoices.csv` | Identify completed bookings (`status == 'completed'`) or completed videos where no matching invoice exists in `invoices.csv`. | Must verify that work was genuinely completed and chargeable before adding. Template `amount: 0` is a placeholder. |
| `never_billed_total` | `number` (`0`) | Total dollar value of completed chargeable unbilled work. | Computed from `never_billed` array | $\sum \text{amount}$ across all `never_billed` records. | Invariant: must equal the sum of line items in `never_billed`. Template `0` is a placeholder. |
| `billing_tool_is_wrong` | `array of objects` (`[{"invoice": "INV-0000", "tool_says": "", "truth": "", "how_you_know": ""}]`) | Explicit discrepancies where the billing software (`invoices.csv`) reports incorrect status or amounts. | `invoices.csv` vs `payments.csv` | Discrepancy comparison: e.g., tool marks invoice as `unpaid` when a bank payment exists, or tool marks invoice as `paid` when no payment was received. | Must provide explicit transactional proof in `how_you_know` (payment ID, date, amount). |
| `lost_requests` | `array of objects` (`[{"video_id": "V000", "request_date": "YYYY-MM-DD", "came_by": "email \| whatsapp \| voice note \| instagram", "why_it_was_lost": ""}]`) | Client edit requests or feedback that were communicated but neglected or missed during video editing. | `messages.txt` cross-referenced with `production_board.csv` | Match messages requesting edits to specific video IDs, checking if instructions were transferred to `notes_on_board` or acted upon. | `came_by` must specify one of the valid channels (`email`, `whatsapp`, `voice note`, `instagram`). |
| `lost_requests_root_causes` | `array of strings` (`[""]`) | Systemic underlying operational causes explaining why client requests fall through the cracks. | Qualitative and operational synthesis of `messages.txt` and workflow | Root cause analysis (e.g., disparate communication channels, absence of central ticketing, lack of editor tagging). | Array of clear, distinct root-cause statements. Template `[""]` is a placeholder. |
| `lost_requests_started_getting_worse_in` | `string` (`"YYYY-MM"`) | The specific month in which client request failures notably increased in frequency or severity. | Temporal analysis of `messages.txt` and `production_board.csv` | Monthly histogram of missed requests, client revision complaints, or turnaround delays. | Must strictly follow `"YYYY-MM"` format (e.g., `"2026-07"`). |
| `never_replied_leads` | `array of strings` (`["L000"]`) | Identifiers of incoming sales leads who never received any reply from Axeom. | `leads.csv` | Query `leads.csv` where `first_reply` is null, empty, or whitespace. | List of exact `lead_id` strings (e.g., `["L012", "L045"]`). Template `["L000"]` is a placeholder. |
| `most_likely_to_leave` | `object` (`{"client": "", "evidence": [""], "what_to_do_this_week": ""}`) | The single client at highest risk of cancellation or churn, along with supporting data evidence and immediate owner action. | Churn indicator analysis across revisions, sentiment in `messages.txt`, delivery delays, billing friction | Synthesis of negative customer signals: repeated botched edits, unresolved complaints, declining booking frequency. | Action must be concrete and executable during the current week of October 6, 2026. |
| `best_marketing_channel` | `object` (`{"channel": "", "why": ""}`) | The acquisition channel delivering the highest profitability or customer lifetime value. | `leads.csv`, `bookings.csv`, `invoices.csv`, `payments.csv`, `costs.csv` | Revenue and margin analysis per channel (e.g., Website form, Instagram, Google, Referral). | Must distinguish revenue from profit. If channel acquisition costs are missing, rationale must disclose assumptions. |
| `what_this_data_cannot_tell_us` | `array of strings` (`[""]`) | Explicit limitations, gaps, and blind spots in the provided dataset. | Cross-functional data audit | Identification of unrecorded variables: marketing ad spend, phone calls without transcripts, client lifetime value beyond export window. | Bulleted array of transparent business data limitations. |
| `monday_top_3` | `array of strings` (`["", "", ""]`) | Exactly three prioritized, high-leverage actions for the studio owner on Monday morning (2026-10-06). | Synthesis of overdue receivables, churn risks, and high-value unanswered leads | Ranked action planning based on cash urgency, client retention impact, and immediate sales opportunity. | Must contain exactly 3 concise, actionable strings. |
